📖 How to Use Speaklet

🗣️ One-Way Mode

Use this when you're speaking one language and want to see it translated into another:

  1. Pick your language — Select the language you're speaking (left dropdown)
  2. Pick target language — Select the language to translate into (right dropdown)
  3. Click the green "Start Listening" button — The button will pulse and the app listens through your microphone
  4. Speak clearly — Say your sentence. The app stops automatically when you pause
  5. Click the red "Stop Listening" button — Stops listening and finishes translating what you said
  6. See the translation — Your original words appear on the left with a speaker button (🔊), translation appears on the right with its own speaker button
  7. Click speaker buttons to replay — Click 🔊 next to any text to hear it read aloud in the selected voice
  8. Optional: Enable "Speak translations" — Check the box with the big 🔊 icon to automatically hear translations read aloud in your chosen voice as they appear

🔁 Conversation Mode

Use this when two people speak different languages and want to have a real conversation:

  1. Pick languages — Person A picks their language (left dropdown), Person B picks theirs (right dropdown)
  2. Enable "Read translations aloud" — Check the box with the big 🔊 icon so each person can hear translations automatically
  3. Pick a voice — Choose which voice reads the translations (Google voices are recommended for better quality)
  4. Tap whose turn it is — Click the large toggle button for whoever is about to speak (shows their language name like "🎙️ Spanish (Spain)")
  5. The button will pulse — The active button pulses to show it's listening for that person's speech
  6. Speak one sentence — Say your sentence clearly. The app automatically stops when you pause for 2 seconds
  7. It translates & reads aloud — What you said appears on your side in your language, and the translation appears on the other side in their language and is read aloud
  8. Tap again for the next turn — Click the other person's button when it's their turn to speak. Keep switching back and forth

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Button colors matter — Green "Start Listening" starts listening, red "Stop Listening" stops it
  • Use speaker buttons (🔊) — Click the speaker button next to any text to hear it read aloud in your chosen voice
  • Use Chrome or Edge — Works best on these browsers (Safari has limited speech recognition)
  • Speak clearly & pause between sentences — The app recognizes you better when you pause for a moment
  • Use a quiet room — Background noise makes speech recognition harder
  • Stay close to the mic — Better distance = better accuracy
  • Google voices are better — The voice selector shows all available voices; Google ones tend to sound more natural and are recommended
  • Allow microphone access — Your browser will ask for permission the first time you click the Start Listening button. Click Allow to continue
  • Download offline models — Go to the Offline tab to download languages you use frequently for offline support

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does it work offline?
A: Speech recognition requires downloading language models from the Offline tab (~30-50MB each). Once downloaded, those languages work completely offline. Translation still needs internet.

Q: Is my audio recorded?
A: When using Google's speech recognition (without offline models), your speech is sent to Google's servers. Offline models process audio locally on your device. Translation text goes to the translation service.

Q: Can I use it on my phone?
A: Yes! It works on Android in Chrome. On iPhone, speech recognition is limited in browsers, so desktop/laptop works best.

Q: Why isn't my language listed?
A: We support 50+ languages! If yours isn't showing, it might not have speech recognition or offline models available yet.
